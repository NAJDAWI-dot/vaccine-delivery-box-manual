# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Healthcare and field workers transporting vaccines with the Enhanced Reliability Vaccine Delivery Box (HTU MPEP 2026 student project), plus instructors/judges evaluating the project. They arrive mid-task — packing a box, reacting to an alarm, prepping a trip — often on a phone, sometimes in Arabic, and need to find one procedure fast and trust what it says.

## Product Purpose

A single-file, offline-capable bilingual (EN/AR) user manual for the vaccine delivery box: setup, operation, emergencies, maintenance, troubleshooting, specs. Success = a first-time user finds and completes the right procedure without help, in either language, on any device, and every claim in the manual matches what the prototype actually does.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, credible, calm — with confident editorial typography. Reads like a well-set WHO field document produced by people who care about craft, not like a marketing site. HTU navy + cyan identity.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing-page grammar: hero metrics, gradient text, glassmorphism, badge walls.
- Fake-premium touches: splash loaders with staged progress, particle backgrounds, cursor effects.
- Certification cosplay: green checkmarks and compliance language implying certifications a student prototype doesn't hold.
- Default cream/parchment "document warmth".

## Design Principles

1. **Honest to the prototype** — copy and visuals claim exactly what the device does; compliance items are design targets, not certificates.
2. **The procedure is the interface** — chrome recedes; finding and following steps is the only job. Anything that delays or decorates gets cut.
3. **One system, two directions** — every token works in light and dark, LTR and RTL, English and Arabic, screen and print.
4. **Field-condition legibility** — AA contrast minimum everywhere (AAA for body), large tap targets, keyboard access to every control.
5. **Editorial confidence, clinical restraint** — bold type hierarchy carries the personality; color stays functional (status semantics only).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA floor (AAA body text where feasible). Full keyboard operability, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme` honored, true `dir="rtl"` Arabic support, print-friendly. Users may be gloved, outdoors, or on low-end phones.
